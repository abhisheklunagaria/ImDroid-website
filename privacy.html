01. Introduction

Welcome to Imdroid (“we,” “our,” or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, retain, disclose, and delete your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

In our pursuit of pushing the boundaries of mobile engineering, we treat your privacy as a cornerstone of our architecture, not an afterthought. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

B. Device & Technical Information

We may automatically collect:

  • Detect fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell users’ personal information. We may share data only in the following situations:

We retain user data only for as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Data Type Retention Period
Account Information Until deletion or up to 24mo inactivity
